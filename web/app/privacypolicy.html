NutriMe privacy policy

NutriMe is a personal app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Health data

With your permission, NutriMe reads steps, sleep, resting heart rate and weight from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone). It uses them only to show how your meals and habits line up with how you feel. This data, your meal log and your check-ins are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to the NutriMe website or anyone else, and NutriMe never writes to Health Connect.

Photos and AI

When you take a photo of ingredients or ask for a recipe or summary, the photo or the summary numbers are sent through your own NutriMe website to an AI service (OpenRouter) to be read. Nothing else is sent, and your notes are never sent.

Removing your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes everything it saved. You can withdraw health permissions at any time in Health Connect settings.
