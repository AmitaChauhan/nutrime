How it works

From fridge to fork in under a minute.

01

Snap your ingredients

Take one photo of what's on the counter. NutriMe names each item and estimates how much there is. Edit anything with a tap.

02

See it balanced

Get the nutrition for the whole meal and suggested portions that hit your targets, along with a recipe to cook it.

03

Check in, learn, adjust

Rate your energy, mood, digestion and sleep. NutriMe shows which meals tend to leave you feeling good.

Food meets feeling

Stop guessing which foods work for you.

Every body is different. NutriMe puts your meals next to how you felt afterwards, so you can spot what lifts you up and what drags you down.

Private by design

Your health data stays yours.

Kept on your phone

Your meal log, check-ins and health data are stored on your device, not in someone else's database.

Protected access

Photo reading runs through a private server behind a passcode, so the AI key never sits on your phone.

No ads, no selling

NutriMe is a personal tool. There's no tracking, no ad network and nothing to sell.
